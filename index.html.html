AMLA & PEP — Quick Guide

Not sure why AMLA is required or whether to select PEP Yes / No? Check here.
Actual Agent Portal screen · tap the image to enlarge

Why does PropNex request AMLA information?

This information supports Customer Due Diligence (CDD): identifying and verifying the client and completing required compliance checks. It does not mean the client is suspected of doing something wrong.

What is PEP?

PEP = Politically Exposed Person. It generally refers to someone entrusted with a prominent public function. Relevant family members and close associates may also fall within the PEP definition.

Important: Being a PEP does not mean the person has done anything wrong. It is a compliance / risk classification.

PEP — should I select YES or NO?

YESSelect Yes if the client falls within the PEP definition.
NOSelect No if the client does not fall within the PEP definition.
Not sure? Do not guess. Check with Sales Admin / Compliance before submitting.
